**Suggested Project Title: GPE E2C Vendor ID Trimming Alignment**

**Context**

**Background**

HBCN provides an English-to-Chinese (E2C) mapping service in GPE for selected customers whose systems do not support Chinese characters. The service enables payment instructions submitted using English-character identifiers to be enriched with predefined beneficiary information, including the beneficiary name, account and bank details.

The customer and the bank jointly maintain an E2C mapping table. GPE identifies the relevant mapping record using the Debit Account, Beneficiary ID (vendor ID) and Credit Account. The customer supplies the vendor ID in the first line of the Beneficiary Name field, which supports up to 35 characters.

Currently, the vendor ID is trimmed when the mapping table is transferred to GPE. However, equivalent trimming is not applied when GPE reads the first line of the Beneficiary Name from an incoming payment instruction. Consequently, a trailing space in that line can prevent a match with the corresponding mapping record.

This longstanding inconsistency has become more apparent as MX usage increases. During message conversion, beneficiary information may be split across multiple lines, leaving a space at the end of the first line. Affected payments require manual intervention.

**Business Drivers**

The main drivers for this request are:

- **Customer experience:** Avoid unnecessary payment processing delays caused by trailing-space mismatches.
- **Operational efficiency:** Reduce manual investigation and repair of payments affected by this issue.
- **Processing accuracy:** Reduce the risk of errors associated with manual handling.
- **Future service usage:** Address the issue before additional customers and increased MX usage create further exposure.
- **Customer retention:** Improve service reliability and reduce the risk of customer dissatisfaction and potential attrition.

**Effect of Drivers**

If no action is taken, payments affected by trailing-space mismatches will continue to require manual intervention, extending processing times and consuming operational resources.

As more customers use the E2C service and MX payment messages, the volume of affected payments may increase. Continued manual handling creates avoidable operational risk, while recurring delays may reduce customer confidence in the service and adversely affect customer retention and future adoption.

**Objectives**

**Vision**

Provide a reliable E2C mapping service that processes eligible customer payments automatically and consistently, without manual intervention caused solely by trailing spaces in the vendor ID.

**Goals**

- Align the trimming of incoming vendor IDs with the existing trimming rules applied to the E2C mapping table.
- Eliminate E2C matching failures caused solely by trailing spaces in the first line of the Beneficiary Name.
- Reduce associated manual processing and payment delays.
- Maintain correct matching behaviour for valid vendor IDs and genuine mismatches.
- Support continued use of the E2C service by existing and future customers.

**Proposed Project**

**Project Summary**

This change will update GPE’s E2C processing so that the vendor ID extracted from the first line of the incoming Beneficiary Name is trimmed before matching against the E2C mapping table.

The trimming behaviour must be consistent with the existing rules applied when mapping-table vendor IDs are transferred to GPE. The change will address trailing-space mismatches while preserving the existing matching criteria and valid vendor ID content.

IT services will include confirmation of the existing trimming rules, impact assessment, development, testing support, deployment and post-implementation verification.

**Project Scope**

In scope:

- Extracting the vendor ID from the first line of the Beneficiary Name using the existing first-line selection logic.
- Applying trimming before E2C matching, consistent with the mapping-table trimming rules.
- Verifying that trailing spaces no longer prevent an otherwise valid match.
- Confirming that Debit Account and Credit Account remain part of the existing matching criteria.
- Regression testing existing successful matches and genuine mismatches.

Out of scope:

- Combining multiple Beneficiary Name lines to form a vendor ID.
- Changing the 35-character first-line limit.
- Removing or altering spaces within a vendor ID.
- Changing customer mapping records or the mapping-table maintenance process.
- Redesigning MX message conversion or other payment processing functions.
- Changing exception handling for genuine mapping failures.
