As part of the Comprehensive Review of Telecom Fraud-related Local Regulatory Requirements, an MSII was identified in relation to ECDS, specifically its CAD commercial draft functionality. Our review of the relevant regulatory documents shows that, since 2024, regulators have issued multiple risk alerts relating to CAD. Inadequate controls over CAD could expose the functionality to misuse by criminals for money laundering, illicit asset transfers and other financial crime activities.
