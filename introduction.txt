We received an inquiry regarding several failed incoming payments via IBPS (Internet Banking Payment System), where the transactions were rejected with the error message “Counterparty Rejection / Digital Signature Verification Failed”.

Following investigation, we identified that the issue occurred during the weekend when HSBC logged out of and subsequently reconnected to the IBPS system. During this period, five banks happened to send their respective key update messages while HSBC was offline or reconnecting. As a result, HSBC did not receive and update the latest keys from these banks in time, which subsequently caused digital signature verification failures for payments initiated by these banks.

The five impacted banks are A, B, C, D and E. Other banks were not affected by this issue.

The relevant keys have now been successfully updated and the issue has been resolved.

Please inform the relevant teams accordingly. If you receive any related enquiries or failed payment cases, please ask the customer to re-initiate the payment.
