After selecting a client on the first-page search, the second page presents the AI-enabled features, highlighted in purple. These include conversational analysis of the charts, anomaly and driver identification, forecasting, opportunity discovery, and recommended action points based on the selected client data.

The key characteristics of the solution are:

1. Deep GPS client intelligence — By combining revenue, transaction-level and client-tag data, we create a comprehensive GPS view of each client. GPS’s unique access to granular payment data enables transaction reconstruction and analysis of key payment attributes. Same-name transfers reveal competitor-bank activity, while transaction narratives and counterparty flows uncover pipeline and NCA opportunities.

2. From data analysis to intelligent forecasting — AI identifies anomalies, key drivers and emerging patterns, forecasts client trends and potential opportunities, and translates them into clear, actionable insights and recommended actions.

3. Replacing complex navigation with conversation — AI brings previously fragmented data and analytical capabilities into one interface. Users can simply ask questions to analyze clients, investigate anomalies, understand drivers, discover opportunities and plan actions—powered by the structured data that GPS has accumulated and continuously governed.

GPS Client 360 uses unique transaction-level data and same-name transfer analysis to reveal client activities with competitor banks and uncover pipeline and NCA opportunities. AI makes these insights easier to access through simple, conversational questions.
